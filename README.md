# pdmx-piano

Solo piano scores that their uploaders published on [MuseScore.com](https://musescore.com) as public
domain or CC0, chosen from [PDMX](https://zenodo.org/records/15571083) (Long, Novack, Berg-Kirkpatrick
and McAuley, "PDMX: A Large-Scale Public Domain MusicXML Dataset for Symbolic Music Processing",
ICASSP 2025; the dataset is CC BY 4.0) for a sight-reading trainer's catalogue of free scores.

A score is here when PDMX's public and internal licence data agree, it is PDMX's best upload of its
piece (one upload per piece), it has a single part and that part is a piano, it opens with two staves
and something to play, its composer died before 1931 (so the music is public domain
everywhere) or the uploader marked the piece as their own composition, and its text claims no copyright
for anyone but the uploader. In all: 6221 scores by 239 listed composers and 1372 uploader's own pieces; 6221 scores in this repository.

- `scores/<d>/<dd>/<id>.mxl`: compressed MusicXML, in PDMX's own folders and names (IPFS content ids).
- `index.json`: one line per score: `path`; `title`; `composer`, a key of the trainer's
  `scripts/composers.ts` (null for an uploader's own piece), and `by`, the composer's name as shown;
  `original`; `license` as MuseScore.com names it; `rating` and `ratings` there; `id`,
  `uploader` and `url` there.

Each file keeps the licence its uploader chose, named in `index.json` and at its `url`. This
selection and index are released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Generated by `scripts/curate-pdmx.ts` in the trainer's repository; the command in its header
regenerates it.
